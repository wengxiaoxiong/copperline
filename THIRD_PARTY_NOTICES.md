# Third-party code

## BLACKWATER — Silent Harbor

Source: https://github.com/Hiraeth010/blackwater (retrieved 2026-09-11)
License: MIT, retained at src/vendor/blackwater/LICENSE.

- `src/vendor/blackwater/weapon.ts`: original procedural MR-17 geometry and effects; attachment type widened to Object3D and hands exposed for third-person use.
- `src/vendor/blackwater/audio.ts`: Soundscape extracted from engine.ts.
- `src/combat.ts` and shooting in `src/game.ts`: adapted MR-17 cadence, recoil, reload duration, damage and effect behavior; original third-person integration.

Three.js and Rapier retain their respective package licenses. All city, vehicle and character geometry is generated in this project. No GTA game assets are distributed.
